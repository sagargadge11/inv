How to create an invoice

Make a professional invoice in a few minutes. No sign-up needed.

  1. Choose language and currencyUse the panel on the right. All labels and amounts switch instantly.
  2. Add your logoClick “Add logo” above the title and pick an image (up to 3 MB).
  3. Fill in the partiesEnter your details under “From” and your customer’s under “Bill To”.
  4. Set number and datesGive the invoice a number, a date and a due date.
  5. Add line itemsDescribe each item or service with a quantity and rate. Use “Add item” for more rows.
  6. Apply discount, tax and shippingEnter percentages for discount and tax, plus any shipping or amount already paid. Totals update live.
  7. Add notes and termsPayment instructions, late fees or a thank-you message.
  8. Download the PDFClick “Download PDF”. The invoice is also saved to your History.

Tips

Use clear, consecutive invoice numbers, state the payment method in the terms, and double-check the currency before downloading.
